Pixel boxes use (left, top, right, bottom), with right/bottom exclusive.
The overview crops preserve original layer colors, geometry, scale bars and
the dashed detail-window marker. Complete original figures remain available
through the website's image links. Cropping and encoding are the only changes.
The two wavefront panels preserve their original panel titles, data, colors,
labels, arrows, timing cells and axes. Their shared original figure title is
provided by the page caption. The complete original wavefront PNG is retained.

The implementation metrics table transcribes cell/core areas and the 20 ns
constraint from supplement/physical20/metrics.json. Figure provenance and
rendering selection are recorded in the upstream layout_manifest.json.

license files alongside the project LICENSE.txt.
